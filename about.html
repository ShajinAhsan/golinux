About GoLinux

An ultra-lightweight Alpine Linux machine, emulated entirely in your browser.

What this is

GoLinux boots a 32-bit Alpine Linux 3.21 (x86) system with BusyBox and TinyCC inside your browser using v86, an x86 emulator written in WebAssembly. There is no server process: the machine runs on your device. The terminal is xterm.js.

Networking is disabled. Your files live in this browser's storage and are never uploaded. They survive reloads and closed tabs; clearing site data or using Reset removes them.
